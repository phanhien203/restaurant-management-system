## Story

**As a** manager, **I want** a basic sales report, **so that** I know how the day went.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. For a date range: revenue, number of bills, average bill value, discounts, revenue by payment method.
2. Sales by item and by category (quantity and revenue); share of QR vs. waiter orders.
3. Export to CSV/Excel; times shown in local time zone.
