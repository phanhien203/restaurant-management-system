# US-20 Item availability (sold out)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Epic | EP-02 Menu |
| Priority | Must |
| Estimate | 2 SP |
| Depends on | [US-06](US-06-realtime-service.md), [US-18](US-18-manage-menu-items.md) |

## Story

**As a** manager, **I want** to mark items sold out or available, **so that** guests cannot order what the kitchen cannot make.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Toggle from the back-office item list; change reaches guest menus and Waiter App within 2 s.
2. Sold-out items are shown greyed out and cannot be added; carts holding them are blocked at submit with a clear message (BR-4).
