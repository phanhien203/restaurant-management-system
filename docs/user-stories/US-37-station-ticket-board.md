# US-37 Station ticket board

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Epic | EP-06 Kitchen Display (KDS) |
| Priority | Must |
| Estimate | 5 SP |
| Depends on | [US-06](US-06-realtime-service.md), [US-33](US-33-confirm-or-reject-qr-orders.md) |

## Story

**As a** kitchen/bar staff member, **I want** tickets for my station on screen, **so that** I prepare the right items in order.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. KDS device is logged in to one or more stations; each confirmed round appears as a ticket with table, round number, time, items, modifiers and notes.
2. Tickets ordered oldest first; new tickets arrive with a sound within 2 s.
3. Layout readable on a tablet or TV from 1.5 m.
