## Story

**As a** platform admin, **I want** to import a restaurant's menu and give staff a quick guide, **so that** a pilot restaurant goes live in a day.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Import categories, items and prices from a CSV template (with validation report).
2. One-page guides (vi) for waiter, kitchen and cashier; go-live checklist (devices, Wi-Fi, QR printed, test order).
