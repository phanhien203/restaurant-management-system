## Story

**As a** guest, **I want** to request a VAT invoice, **so that** my company can claim the expense.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Guest (on phone) or cashier enters company name, tax code (validated format), address, email.
2. Details are linked to the bill and shown on the cashier screen.
