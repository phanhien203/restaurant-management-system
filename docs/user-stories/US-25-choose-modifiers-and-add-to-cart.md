| Priority | Must |

## Story

**As a** guest, **I want** to pick options and add a note, **so that** my dish is made the way I want.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Item sheet shows photo, description, allergens, modifier groups and a note field.
2. Required groups and min/max rules are enforced before "Add"; price updates with modifiers and quantity.
