## Story

**As a** waiter, **I want** to see all tables and their status live, **so that** I know where I'm needed.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Tables grouped by zone with status: Empty, Occupied, Waiting for food, Bill requested, Cleaning; pending requests badged.
2. Tapping a table opens its session: rounds, item statuses, guest count.
3. Usable on phone and tablet; updates within 2 s.
