## Story

**As a** Tenant Admin, **I want** to configure my branch, **so that** bills and the QR page match my restaurant.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Service charge %, VAT % and whether menu prices include VAT.
2. VietQR bank account: bank (BIN), account number, account name.
3. QR-order confirmation alert threshold (minutes, default 3).
4. Logo and primary colour for the guest page; restaurant name and address for bills.
