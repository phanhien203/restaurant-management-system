## Story

**As a** guest, **I want** to see my order status and the table's running bill, **so that** I know what's coming and what we owe.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Items show: Waiting for confirmation → Confirmed → Preparing → Ready → Served, or Rejected with reason; updated live.
2. Running bill lists all confirmed items of the session with totals.
